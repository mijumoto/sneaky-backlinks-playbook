# Strategies

## 1. Parasite SEO (Connor Showler)
Publish content on high-authority UGC and publishing platforms so your brand/content ranks for long-tails and gets cited by AI systems.

### Strong platforms repeatedly recommended
- Reddit, Quora, Medium, Substack, Hashnode, LinkedIn
- Ghost, Beehiiv, Blogger, Tumblr
- Imgur, SlideShare, Calameo
- Digg, Bluesky, Threads
- Vercel (subdomains), Discord (emerging)
- Pinterest, YouTube, X, Facebook Groups

### Tactics
- Front-load titles and early paragraphs with target keywords.
- Seed brand name throughout.
- Link back to your site where possible (especially on owned accounts).
- Build tiered links to the parasite posts for indexing.
- Syndicate the same content across multiple platforms.

## 2. Agency & Client Mining
- Identify top SEO agencies in your niche.
- Pull their (and their clients’) backlink profiles in Ahrefs / SEMrush.
- Look for high-DR profile, directory, and Web 2.0 links that are not already in the collection.
- Prioritize aged domains with real traffic.

## 3. Volume Foundation
Build a large number of free or low-friction links from:
- Social / professional profiles
- Link-in-bio tools
- Web 2.0 site builders
- General and niche directories
- Package registries and software listings

## 5. Quality Filters
- Prefer DR 70+.
- Prefer dofollow or mixed when possible.
- Prefer platforms with real organic traffic and indexing.
- Avoid pure spam directories with no traffic.
- Note link type and verification status.
