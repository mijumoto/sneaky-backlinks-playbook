# Search Techniques

## Google Search Operators
```
"submit your site" OR "add your website" OR "list your business" directory
intitle:directory OR intitle:"add url" OR intitle:"submit site"
inurl:directory OR inurl:listing OR inurl:profile
"powered by" OR "created with" site:wordpress.com OR site:blogspot.com
site:medium.com OR site:substack.com OR site:hashnode.dev
"link in bio" OR "website" site:about.me OR site:linktr.ee
```

## Platform-specific
- GitHub: search for awesome lists, package registries, profile websites.
- Product Hunt / BetaList style directories.
- Local citation sites (Yelp, BBB, Google Business Profile).
- Academic: Academia.edu, ResearchGate, ORCID.
- Creative: Dribbble, Behance, DeviantArt, Instructables.

## Tools
- Ahrefs / SEMrush: backlink profiles of agencies and competitors.
- Google Maps: local SEO agencies → their clients’ profiles.
- Similarweb / traffic tools: filter for real traffic.
- Manual: browse “best free backlinks 2026” and “high DR Web 2.0” lists, then verify.

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Live DR / DA check
- [ ] Link type (dofollow / nofollow / mixed) on a real page
- [ ] Indexing status of profile/listing pages
- [ ] Traffic or ranking evidence
- [ ] Not already in the MongoDB collection
